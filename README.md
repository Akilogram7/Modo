# Modo

Modo is a one‑tap life automation app that instantly switches your phone into whatever mode you need. Tap Study to silence notifications, open your notes, and start a focus timer. Tap Sleep to dim your screen, set alarms, and block distracting apps. Tap Commute to see weather, bus times, and your playlist. 
These settings are completely customizable to the user, they can choose to connect whichever apps/settings they want to with Modo and then automate their routine. You can trigger modes with a button or voice command, allowing for quick and easy access.
